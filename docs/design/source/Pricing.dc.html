Questions

Will Apple accept an app made from my website?

Apple rejects apps that are only a website in a frame. Mobilio adds native tabs, push notifications, an offline screen and a native launch experience, and checks your app before submission. If Apple asks for changes, we help you fix them and resubmit.

Do I need to update the app when my website changes?

No. Content comes from your website, so new products and prices show up right away. You only rebuild when you change the icon, tabs or app settings.

Who owns the app?

You do. It's published under your Apple Developer account, with your name as the seller.

What happens if I cancel?

Your app stays on the App Store and keeps showing your website. You can't ship new versions or send push notifications until you subscribe again.
