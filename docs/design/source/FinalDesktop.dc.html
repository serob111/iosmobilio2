No. You do everything in the browser. We build and sign the app on our side.

Only to publish on the App Store. The preview and the iPhone test work without one.

What if Apple rejects my app?+

We explain the reason in plain words, make the changes with you and submit again.

Will changes on my site show up in the app?+

Yes, right away. A new build is only needed when you change the icon, name or tabs.
